ARCHDRAGONS

An original 5E-compatible creature.

The only certainty regarding an Archdragon is its unpredictability.

Nature and Characteristics

Archdragons are extraordinary dragons distinguished above all by their individuality. No two need share the same temperament, disposition, alignment, intentions, or ambitions. An Archdragon may be benevolent or malevolent, lawful or chaotic, solitary or surrounded by companions. Its nature cannot be reliably inferred from its appearance, previous actions, or circumstances.

The unpredictability of an Archdragon is intrinsic. It is not randomness, indecision, instability, or ignorance. An Archdragon may respond differently to identical circumstances, even moments apart. Knowledge of its history, relationships, purposes, and previous behavior provides no reliable means of anticipating its next action.

An Archdragon's purposes may be temporary or enduring. It may pursue an ambition for centuries, abandon it without warning, or adopt an entirely different purpose. The duration of its interests cannot be predicted.

Physical Characteristics

An Archdragon possesses four limbs, two wings, and a long tail. Its forelimbs are dexterous, five-fingered, scaled hands ending in talons. These hands are capable of manipulating objects and wielding weapons. Its hind limbs are larger and more powerful.

Its coloration may combine characteristics commonly associated with both chromatic and metallic dragons. No particular coloration indicates temperament, alignment, or disposition.

Archdragons are capable of articulate speech. Their languages depend upon their Intelligence.

Magic

An Archdragon has no innate magical abilities. Its capacity to learn, employ, and teach magic is inherent, but its actual knowledge and proficiency must be acquired.

An Archdragon may learn spells through study and experience, use magic items, and teach magic to others when it possesses the necessary knowledge. Its spellcasting follows the ordinary rules for components and concentration.

Each Archdragon has an individual Spellcasting Level (SL), independent of any character class. Instead of spell slots, it uses the Spell Points variant. Its spell point pool equals half its SL, rounded up (⌈SL ÷ 2⌉). For example, SL 1 or 2 provides 1 spell point; SL 3 or 4 provides 2; SL 5 or 6 provides 3. Spell points are spent to cast spells using the standard Spell Points variant rules.

An Archdragon may use spells to assume other forms, but shapeshifting is not an inherent ability.

Weapons and Combat

Archdragons can employ simple weapons, provided the weapon is no larger than the Archdragon itself. Weapon damage and ability modifiers follow the ordinary rules for the weapon.

An Archdragon's combat approach is not prescribed. It may employ physical attacks, weapons, its breath weapon, spells, or combinations of these capabilities. Weapon attacks are not combined with its claw-and-bite multiattack routine.

Archdragons possess no inherent damage resistances or immunities based solely on their species.

Breath Weapon

An Archdragon can select one of five elements whenever it uses its breath weapon: fire, lightning, acid, water, or wind.

It can manifest the breath as either a beam (5 feet wide and 30 feet long) or a cone (15 feet).

Fire, lightning, and acid use Dexterity saving throws. Water and wind beams use Dexterity saving throws; water and wind cones use Strength saving throws.

Water and wind may also produce a separate Dexterity saving throw for their knockdown effect. Huge and Gargantuan creatures are unaffected by that knockdown effect.

Fire may ignite susceptible materials. Water may engulf or submerge creatures for up to 1 minute.

Lairs and Treasure

An Archdragon's lair is wherever it chooses to establish one, for as long as it chooses to remain there. Its lair may be an uninviting cavern, a welcoming great hall, or virtually any other environment. Its surroundings do not reliably indicate its temperament or intentions.

An Archdragon may relocate its lair at any time.

Its hoard may contain currency, art, magical objects, historical treasures, magical knowledge, curiosities, and other possessions. Its contents are individual and unpredictable.

Social Behavior

An Archdragon may live in complete solitude or maintain an extensive entourage. It may associate with other dragons, other creatures, or no one at all. There is no prescribed social structure or customary relationship between Archdragons.

Lair Actions

Adult, Ancient, and Greatwyrm Archdragons can employ lair actions. These are governed by a flexible framework rather than a fixed sequence or predetermined menu. The DM determines appropriate effects in keeping with the environment, the Archdragon's intentions, and the circumstances.

Possible manifestations include environmental, elemental, spatial, magical, and defensive effects. Lair actions should remain appropriate to the situation rather than granting arbitrary or unlimited power. Archdragons do not possess Legendary Actions at any age.

ARCHDRAGON WYRMLING

Small Dragon, Any Alignment

Age: 0–5 years | CR 10 (5,900 XP) | Proficiency Bonus: +4

AC: 10 | Initiative: +2 (12) | HP: 89 (12d8 + 35) | Speed: 30 ft., fly 60 ft.

STR 19 (+4) | DEX 14 (+2) | CON 17 (+3) | INT 14 (+2) | WIS 11 (+0) | CHA 16 (+3)

Skills: Perception +4, Stealth +6 | Senses: Blindsight 10 ft., Darkvision 60 ft.

Languages: Common, Draconian; 8 workweeks and 200 GP to learn a new language.

Spellcasting: Individual SL; spell point pool follows the Archdragon Spell Points rule. Can learn and cast spells of up to 1st level. SL reflects its own magical study and experience.

Bite: Melee attack; 1d10+4 piercing damage only.

Breath Weapon (Recharge 5–6): Chooses one of the five available elements and either a beam or cone manifestation. Damage at this age is 7d6. Other elemental damage and secondary effects follow the Archdragon Breath Weapon rules.
