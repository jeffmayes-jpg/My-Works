Personal Deism

A philosophical worldview affirming an intentional, good Divine reality while leaving individuals free to pursue truth through reason, spiritual insight, and personal reflection. Persons possess intrinsic worth, sentient beings deserve fundamental freedoms, and moral growth is the purpose of personal existence.

Babel

A multilingual text-to-speech application supporting English, French, Spanish, and Khmer. It includes a language-learning curriculum organized by grade level and category, and allows users to add their own words and phrases.

BirthPersonaReport

A Windows desktop application built with VB.NET, Windows Forms, and .NET 10. It presents several traditional and interpretive systems in a tabbed, person-specific report.
